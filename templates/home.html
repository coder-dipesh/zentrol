PIPELINE
— 01 / Capabilities

Quietly capable.
Loudly useful.

Each capability is on by default — nothing to configure, no settings sprawl. The full feature set is shipped with the launch tier.

01

Real-time hand tracking that keeps up with the room.

MediaPipe runs in-browser at roughly twenty-four milliseconds per frame, so a flick of the wrist is read instantly — not after a beat.

02

Reads the actual text on every slide — not a script.

Narration is generated from the contents of your deck, slide by slide. Edit a bullet at 2 a.m., and the next dry-run reads what you wrote.

03

Drops into Moodle as an LTI 1.3 activity.

Add Zentrol once at the site level; instructors embed it as an external tool. Single sign-on, gradebook passback, and per-course launch — out of the box.

04

No clickers, no dongles, no installs.

Anything with a webcam and a modern browser will do. Camera frames are processed locally — they never touch a server.

— 02 / Setup

From a fresh tab to hands-free in three steps.

Setup happens once. After that, every deck you upload picks up the same camera and narration profile.

  1. 01

    Upload

    Drop your .pptx in.

    Parsed locally — your deck never leaves the browser tab.

  2. 02

    Allow camera

    Wave once to calibrate.

    A four-second handshake teaches the model the lighting in your room.

  3. 03

    Present

    Step away from the keyboard.

    Swipe to advance, palm to pause narration, fist to blank the screen.
